How I work

Each of these is a habit I can point at a document for, not a quality I am claiming about myself.

  • I plan before I build

    Before recording a frame of my Unit 7 video I wrote a treatment setting out the purpose, audience and runtime, a ten-section script, an eight-panel storyboard and a four-week schedule. The edit followed the plan, and where it did not I wrote down why.

    See the planning documents

  • I assess risk before I start

    My Unit 7 risk assessment lists six hazards scored for severity and likelihood, each with named controls: taping down cables, ventilating equipment, surge protectors, backing up to cloud storage, and building a realistic schedule.

    See the risk assessment

  • I test against a plan

    For Unit 13 I wrote a five-test plan covering navigation, form submission, multimedia playback, responsiveness and external links, then tested the built site against it, repaired the faults and documented what changed.

    See the test plan

  • I reference where things came from

    My Unit 7 asset list has fourteen entries, each with a source reference, and my Unit 13 table of sources records the licence terms for every image, icon, sound and video used. It costs ten minutes and it means nobody has to take my word for it.

    See the table of sources

  • I act on feedback and say what changed

    Feedback on my video said it needed clearer speech, a slightly shorter length and better clarity in places. I cut unnecessary sections, improved the audio and refined the commentary, and recorded that in my evaluation rather than just agreeing with it.

    See the evaluation

  • I check my own work against the source

    Writing this portfolio up I found two rows of my own risk assessment where the printed rating does not equal severity multiplied by likelihood, and a scene in my shooting log dated 28/20/25. They are on the page, marked as errors, because hiding them would be the worse choice.

    See what I found
