Coursework

Seven units. Three of them have work you can open and look at. Two are briefs I have not started yet, and two are exam units with no coursework at all. Every row below says which it is.

I have labelled each unit with what stage it is genuinely at. It would be easy to write all seven up as if they were finished projects, and the earlier version of this site did exactly that, including for units where the only file I have is the assignment brief. Being straight about which is which seemed more useful to anyone reading this, and it means the three that are finished carry more weight.
